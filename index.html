Do you need my Epic Games password for anything? ▾

No — and there is no version of “account verification” that requires it. A password is only ever typed by you, on Epic’s own site, during the official sign-in flow (see the next question). Our site has no password field at all and cannot accept one.

If anyone — including someone claiming to be from fortyworth, Epic support, or a “verification bot” — asks you to hand over your password or a 2FA code, it is phishing. Real Epic Games staff will never ask either.

How does “Sign in with Epic Games” work? ▾

It uses OAuth, the same official mechanism used by thousands of apps:

  1. You click the Sign in with Epic Games button.
  2. Your browser is redirected to accounts.epicgames.com — the official Epic Games domain. You enter your credentials there, on Epic’s page, not on ours.
  3. Epic asks which permissions the app requests and you approve them.
  4. Epic sends our site a limited access token. It identifies your account (display name / account ID) — your password never leaves Epic’s servers, because you never type it on our site.

You can revoke access at any time: Epic account → Connections → Apps → disconnect the app.

How do I check that I’m on the real Epic login page? ▾

Look at the address bar before typing anything. The only legitimate Epic login domain is https://accounts.epicgames.com (note the epicgames.com part and the padlock).

Lookalike domains — for example ep1c-games-login.com, epicgames.verify-account.xyz, fortyworth-login.net — are fakes. If a site shows a login form on its own page instead of sending you to epicgames.com, do not type anything. When in doubt, open epicgames.com manually and sign in from there.

Someone asked for my password “to verify my account”. What do I do? ▾

Refuse and report. No legitimate service verifies accounts through passwords.

If you already shared it:

  1. Change your Epic password immediately at epicgames.com.
  2. Enable two-factor authentication (see below).
  3. Sign out of all sessions: Account → Password & Security → sign out everywhere.
  4. Check Connections → Apps and remove anything you do not recognize.
What is the Pirate Token? ▾

The community name for the Pirate Shop Access Token — the event currency of the Treasure Hunters mini-season. You earn it by claiming high-tier Buried Pirate Chests and spend it in the pirate-themed shop. Our step-by-step guide lives in docs/how-to-get-pirate-token-in-fortnite.md.

What is Treasure Hour? ▾

A limited window during which the Treasure Hour badge goes live and high-tier chest odds improve. The live tracker is on the fortyworth.com homepage; confirmed schedule reports per region are collected in docs/treasure-hour-schedule.md.

How do I secure my Epic account? ▾

Four steps, five minutes:

  1. Turn on 2FA: epicgames.com → log in → Account → Password & Security → Two-Factor Authentication. An authenticator app is the strongest option; email and SMS also work.
  2. Use a unique password that you do not reuse anywhere else.
  3. Never enter your password anywhere except epicgames.com.
  4. Review Connections → Apps now and then and remove apps you no longer use.

Account safety checklist

1. Never share your password

Not with “admins”, “support”, “verification bots”, streamers, or friends. Nobody legitimate will ever ask.

2. Check the domain

The only real Epic login page is accounts.epicgames.com. Everything else that asks for credentials is fake.
