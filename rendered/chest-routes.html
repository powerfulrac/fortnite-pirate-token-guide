Fortnite Buried Pirate Chest Routes — Crossbones Island Guide (2026)

Fortnite Buried Pirate Chest Routes — Crossbones Island Guide (2026)

During an active Treasure Hour window, every eligible container you open is a boosted roll for the Pirate Shop Access Token. Routes with the highest concentration of Buried Pirate Chests and Rare Blue Chests give you the most rolls per match.

Guide updated: November 2026.

Route comparison

Route Name Primary POIs Best Mode Avg. High-Tier Chests
Coastal Cave Run Smuggler's Cove Cliffs Zero Build Solos 8 – 10 Chests
Shoreline Wreckage Sunken Galleon → South Beach Duos / Squads 10 – 14 Chests
Lagoon Geyser Chain North Lagoon → Pirate Outpost Ranked / Pubs 7 – 9 Chests

Coastal Cave Run — Smuggler's Cove Cliffs

The solo-friendly pick. Work the sea caves along Smuggler's Cove — they hold Buried Pirate Chests and get checked far less often than open beaches. Zero Build keeps you alive while you clear the cave line, and the route loops back on itself, so a clean run touches 8–10 high-tier chests inside a single window. Best mode: Zero Build Solos.

Shoreline Wreckage — Sunken Galleon to South Beach

The densest rotation on Crossbones Island and the default choice when you are queued with friends. Loot the Sunken Galleon wrecks first, then sweep South Beach — Duos and Squads can split the wreck and beach lanes and still meet before the zone closes. Expect 10–14 high-tier chests per full run. Best mode: Duos / Squads.

Lagoon Geyser Chain — North Lagoon to Pirate Outpost

The competitive pick. Chain the watchtowers around North Lagoon, cut through the geysers, and finish at the Pirate Outpost. Slightly fewer containers (7–9) but strong positioning for the late game, which is why it is the route of choice in Ranked. Best mode: Ranked / Pubs.

Read the live heatmap before you drop

The FortyWorth Live Tracker heatmap breaks each route down by POI — sea caves, wreck sites, and watchtowers — and the community drop feed shows where tokens were claimed most recently. If a route is marked hot on your region, take the second-densest rotation instead: an uncontested 8-chest run beats a contested 14-chest run almost every time.
