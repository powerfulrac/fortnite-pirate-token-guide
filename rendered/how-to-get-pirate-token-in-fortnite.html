How to Get the Pirate Token in Fortnite (2026 Step-by-Step Guide)

Quick answer: open the FortyWorth Live Tracker, run the free locker scan, check your locker's worth, queue into a match during an active Treasure Hour window, clear a high-density Buried Pirate Chest route, and claim your Pirate Shop Access Token. The token unlocks eligible Item Shop cosmetics for 14 days — and everything you claim during that window stays in your locker permanently.

Guide updated: November 2026.

The Pirate Shop Access Token is the reward of the Fortnite: Treasure Hunters mini-season. Getting one by brute force means dropping into the most contested POIs and rolling against brutal base RNG. This guide walks through the faster, tracker-assisted route that the Fortnite Pirate Token Finder repo is built around — the same flow used by the FortyWorth Live Tracker.

What you need

  • An Epic Games account with Fortnite installed — PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, or Nintendo Switch all work the same way.
  • Any web browser for the tracker. Nothing gets installed on your PC or console, and the tracker never asks for your Epic Games password or any account credentials.

Step 1 — Scan your locker on FortyWorth.com

Open fortyworth.com in any browser and run the free locker scan. The scan reads your publicly visible locker and estimates its worth in V-Bucks and USD — skins, emotes, backblings, the whole inventory. No login, no Epic credentials, works on mobile too.

Step 2 — Check your locker's worth

Right after the scan you get your estimated locker value. This is also how you confirm the scan worked before you spend time in a match. Want a deeper breakdown and a shareable value card? See How to Check Your Fortnite Locker Value & Account Worth.

Step 3 — Watch the Treasure Hour countdown

On the main dashboard, check the Treasure Hour status badge and the live countdown timer. Treasure Hour windows are scheduled server-side multiplier windows when Pirate Token drop rates spike — the single biggest factor in how fast you get the token. There is no fixed public schedule that stays true for every server, so trust the live badge, not stale third-party time lists. Details: Treasure Hour Schedule & Live Timer.

Step 4 — Run a high-density chest route during the window

Queue the moment the badge turns live and run one of the three Crossbones Island rotations that concentrate Buried Pirate Chests and Rare Blue Chests. More eligible containers per match = more rolls while drop rates are boosted. The best route for most players is Shoreline Wreckage (10–14 high-tier chests, Duos/Squads). Full breakdown: Chest Routes Guide.

Step 5 — Claim your Pirate Token and open the Item Shop

Once the Pirate Shop Access Token is claimed on your Epic account, open the Fortnite Item Shop and unlock eligible outfits, emotes, and bundles for free within 14 days. Anything you claim during the two-week window remains permanently in your locker — even after the window closes.

If the token does not show up

Work through this checklist in order:

  1. Confirm the Treasure Hour badge was actually live during your match — check the claim time against the dashboard's window history.
  2. Make sure you finished the claim step on the tracker and you are logged into the same Epic account in the game.
  3. Restart the Fortnite client and reopen the Item Shop — eligible items are marked while the token is active.
  4. Still nothing? Open an issue in this repo with your match time and route (see the issue templates) so route data and window reports can be cross-checked.
