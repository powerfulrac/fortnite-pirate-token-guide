How to Check Your Fortnite Locker Value & Account Worth (2026)

Your locker value is the estimated worth of everything in your Fortnite locker — skins, emotes, backblings, pickaxes and the rest — expressed in V-Bucks and USD. In the Treasure Hunters mini-season it is also step one of the Pirate Token flow: the locker scan on the tracker is what kicks off the reward process.

Guide updated: November 2026.

Check it in 30 seconds (free, no login)

  1. Open fortyworth.com in any browser — desktop or mobile.
  2. Run the locker scan. It reads your publicly visible locker; it never asks for your Epic Games password or any account credentials.
  3. Read your estimated worth in V-Bucks and USD, then grab your Pirate Token reward from the same dashboard — see the step-by-step token guide.

What moves your number up or down

Locker worth is dominated by the cosmetics you own and how they were acquired. Battle Pass and Item Shop outfits, rare emotes and backblings add the most; default items add nothing. Two lockers with the same outfit count can differ a lot in value — rarity and source matter more than quantity.

Repeat the scan any time

The scan is free and takes seconds — run it again after every Item Shop purchase or Battle Pass tier to watch your account worth grow over the mini-season. If you share your value somewhere, post the estimate together with a locker screenshot; never share your Epic Games password or any credentials to "verify" a locker — the official flow does not need them.

Quick FAQ

Is the locker scan safe? It runs in the browser and does not request credentials. Never share your Epic Games password with any third-party site — the official token flow does not need it.

Is it free? Yes — the scan, the worth estimate, and the Pirate Token reward flow are all free.

Does my locker value affect the Pirate Token? No — the scan is the entry step of the flow. Every player claims the same token reward.
